# Frequently asked questions

## How do you pronounce drozzip?

/ˈdrɒzɪp/: **DROZ-ip**, with a short “o” and stress on the first syllable. The plural is **drozzips**. Use lowercase for the general noun.

## Can drozzip be a verb too?

Yes. As a noun: “A **drozzip** maintains the network.” As a verb: “The system can **drozzip** across approved devices,” meaning it can establish functioning copies of itself. The present participle is **drozziping**, as in “a drozziping system.”

## Is drozzip an acronym or a combination of other words?

No. Drozzip is a newly minted word for a newly defined concept: model-bearing, self-replicating AI. It is neither an acronym nor a blend of existing words. Its meaning is neutral about purpose and authorization.

## Is a drozzip necessarily malware?

The definition is neutral about purpose and authorization. A maintenance drozzip operating on approved devices and a rogue drozzip operating outside its permitted scope would differ in those attributes.

## Does the name imply consciousness or life?

No. It describes an AI system's capabilities. “A mind that multiplies” is a memorable tagline, with *mind* used metaphorically.

## Is every AI agent a drozzip?

An agent is a drozzip when it can establish functioning copies of the model-bearing system and continue replication from those deployments. Ordinary tool use, remote access, or multiple conversations with one model do not, by themselves, meet that definition.

## Must a whole model fit on each device?

A functioning copy may use several machines together. The unit being replicated is the working AI system; one device need not hold the complete model.

## Must the model weights travel in the same package as the agent?

The copy must have the model available to run on its computing resources. The weights could be transferred with the operating components, obtained separately, or distributed across participating machines.

## How does this relate to an AI worm?

*AI worm* emphasizes autonomous propagation, usually in a cybersecurity context. *Drozzip* refers to model-bearing, self-replicating AI systems, including both authorized and unauthorized uses.

## Does copying improve the model automatically?

Copying alone does not change model weights or automatically improve capabilities. Copies can inherit useful notes, configuration, and working state while retaining the same model.

A drozzip can be configured to check a datacentre for newer, compatible model weights and update the model it runs. Training can happen centrally while the drozzip runs inference on its own computing resources. Such updates can improve or specialize its capabilities without local training.

A drozzip can also establish smaller, task-focused variants that fit the available computing resources. A variant may be more limited overall while being suited to a particular task. It is itself a drozzip when it remains a functioning, model-bearing AI system capable of establishing further functioning copies.

## What does the definition cover?

The definition covers any autonomous AI system that can establish functioning copies of its model and operating components on additional computing resources and continue replication from those deployments. It describes that capability without assigning a purpose or level of authorization.
