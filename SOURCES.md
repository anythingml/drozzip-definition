# Sources and research context

Research context checked on **15 October 2026**. The definition of *mim* in this repository is a proposal, separate from the terminology and conclusions of the sources below.

## Autonomous replication and model-bearing systems

William Zhou and Daniel Freeman, Anthropic Frontier Red Team, **12 October 2026**: [AI models may be capable of self-replicating without access to a datacenter](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai).

The article uses *autonomous replication and adaptation* for a wider research concern and *weight-based replicators* for autonomous worms running models on compromised hardware. It distinguishes those systems from API-based replicators.

The experiments examine inference setup on unfamiliar hardware and simplified replication tasks. They do not establish a small, fully autonomous rogue replicator operating in the wild. The authors' near-term forecast is a projection beyond the experiments.

This proposal uses neutral language so that authorized maintenance and research applications can be discussed alongside unauthorized behavior.

## Existing use of the name

[Dixa: Mim AI Agent](https://www.dixa.com/ai-agent).

Dixa uses **Mim** as a product name for a customer-service AI agent. That existing use is distinct from the proposed general noun **mim** defined in this repository. The proposal does not claim endorsement by Dixa or Anthropic.
